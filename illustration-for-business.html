Handpainted illustrations for local businesses

I paint and draw illustrations by hand, using gouache, watercolour and coloured pencil, and turn them into artwork for local cafés, bakeries, bookshops and more! That might be a menu cover, a poster for your wall, a loyalty card, or stickers and bookmarks. Each piece is custom made for your space, so it feels like it belongs to your business rather than stock art.

How it works

We start with a short chat about your business, what you’d like illustrated and where it will be used. I then send a rough sketch so you can see the direction early. You can give feedback on the sketch, and I refine it until you’re happy. Once you approve it, I paint the final artwork and send you print-ready files.
