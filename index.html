Welcome to my illustrated world

Lulu Xu is a Chinese Australian illustrator and artist currently based in Wellington, New Zealand, with a passion for storytelling woven into every piece of her artwork. Her artistic journey began with a deep appreciation for nature and whimsical themes, which continue to inspire her creativities.

Custom Paintings

Have something you'd love to see painted? I create custom artworks by hand in gouache, watercolour and coloured pencil, in a warm, storybook style. Past commissions include a portrait of two much-loved cats, and I'm happy to paint pets, homes, favourite places, or a scene from a story that matters to you.

Every piece starts with a conversation. Tell me what you have in mind, and I'll come back with a sketch for your approval before I paint the final artwork.
